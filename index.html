App privacy and support information.

Privacy

Your drawings, album, puzzle photos and progress are stored on your device. The app does not send them to our servers. Uninstalling the app may delete this data.

Photos and Camera

For My Puzzles, you can choose a photo or take one using the camera. The app stores up to six photos; adding another replaces the oldest. Deleting or replacing a photo only affects the copy stored by the app. The original photo in your device's gallery is not deleted.

Full Version

You pay once for the full version. If you reinstall the app using the same account you used to pay, your purchased full version will be available automatically.

Using the App

No registration is required to use the app. The app has no advertising. Your artwork and progress are stored on your device.

Support

For help or to report a problem, email smartappclub@gmail.com. Describe what you were doing when the problem occurred. Do not send private photos, passwords or payment card details.
